Section 6 race charts

The left chart shows the mean cost of each algorithm over the 50 instances, iteration by iteration. The right chart shows, for the same algorithms, the share of function-to-variable messages that sit at a bound. Both charts run on one timeline. So you can see that when a line's messages reach the bound, it stops moving.

Try this: pick Random dense, keep k = 1,000 and press Play. By iteration 100, 91% of DMS-SCFG's messages are at a bound, and by iteration 140 its cost is within 0.1% of its final value. DMS-kDS runs exactly as DMS until iteration 1,000. Then it splits: within 50 iterations its share at a bound goes from 61% to 93%, and from iteration 1,028 its cost is below DMS-SCFG.
